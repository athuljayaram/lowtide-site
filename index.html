Common questions

Lowtide can't block apps.

On iPhone, Lowtide needs Screen Time access. Open the app and tap Allow Screen Time access, or check Settings → Screen Time.

On Android, Lowtide needs its Accessibility service turned on. Open the app and tap Turn on app blocking, or go to Settings → Accessibility → Lowtide. If you force-stop Lowtide, Android turns the service off, so turn it back on there.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Open Settings in Lowtide and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings in Lowtide and tap Manage subscription, or go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions on Android.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in Lowtide and tap Delete account and data.
