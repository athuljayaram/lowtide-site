LOWTIDE PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: October 27, 2026

Lowtide helps you block distracting apps and focus. We collect as little as we can, we never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.


WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

The apps you choose to block never leave your device.

- iPhone: blocking is handled by Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple gives Lowtide only private tokens for the apps, categories and websites you pick, not their names.
- Android: Lowtide uses Android's Accessibility service only to see which app is opening, so it can show the block screen during a focus session. It never reads or records what's on your screen. Your list of blocked apps is processed only on your device and is never sent to us.


WHAT WE STORE

- Account ID. A random ID created when you first open the app, so your history and purchases belong to you.
- Email address, only if you create an account with email, Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Apple may give us a private relay address instead.
- Focus sessions. When each session started and ended, its planned length and how many items were blocked. This powers your weekly stats.
- Purchase status. Whether you have Lowtide Pro and which plan. We never see your payment details; Apple or Google handles payment.


WHO PROCESSES IT

- Supabase hosts our database and sign-in.
- RevenueCat manages subscriptions and receipts.
- Apple processes App Store payments and Sign in with Apple.
- Google processes Google Play payments and Sign in with Google.

Each acts only on our behalf and under its own privacy policy.


DELETING YOUR DATA

In the app, open Settings and tap "Delete account and data". This permanently removes your account, focus history and purchase record from our servers. It does not cancel your subscription; cancel that first in your Apple ID settings (iPhone) or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions (Android). You can also email us to delete your account.


CHILDREN

Lowtide is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.


CHANGES

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app for any significant change.
