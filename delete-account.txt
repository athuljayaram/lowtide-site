LOWTIDE: DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT AND DATA
Developer: Securityinfinity Inc.


DELETE IN THE APP

1. Open Lowtide.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap "Delete account and data" and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.


DELETE BY EMAIL

If you can't open the app, email support@securityinfinity.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Lowtide account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.


WHAT IS DELETED

- Your account ID and email address
- Your focus session history
- Your Lowtide Pro purchase record on our servers

Nothing is kept after deletion. The list of apps you block was only ever stored on your phone; uninstall Lowtide to remove it.


SUBSCRIPTIONS

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions (Android) or in your Apple ID settings (iPhone).
